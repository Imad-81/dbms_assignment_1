BR-1 No Overlapping Doctor Appointments
A doctor cannot be scheduled for two concurrent consultations.		 UNIQUE (doctor_id, appointment_date, appointment_time) Double booking and schedule clash anomalies.
BR-2 Exclusive Bed Occupancy
Only one active patient may occupy an inpatient bed at any point in time.		 Bed status tracking: status = 'OCCUPIED' during admission; admission requires available bed. Bed allocation collision and ghost bed vacancies.
BR-3 Chronological Admission Bounds
Discharge timestamp cannot precede admission timestamp.		 CHECK (discharge_date IS NULL OR discharge_date >= admission_date) Negative inpatient stay duration and erroneous billing.
BR-4 Positive Financial Balances
All rates, bill line items, and payment amounts must be non-negative.		 CHECK (amount_paid > 0.00), CHECK (total_amount >= 0.00) Negative billing and ledger distortion.
BR-5 Biological & Demographic Validity
DOB must be in past; vitals must fall within physiological human bounds.		 CHECK (dob <= TODAY), CHECK (temp BETWEEN 30 AND 45) Corrupt clinical history and data entry typos.
BR-6 Strict 1:1 Consultation Linkage
An appointment generates at most one medical encounter record.		 appointment_id INT NOT NULL UNIQUE in consultation table. Duplicate consultation records for a single visit.

Q1: How does the schema achieve Third Normal Form (3NF)?

Answer: Every relation is in 1NF (atomic attributes, no repeating groups) and 2NF (all non-key attributes fully functionally dependent on atomic primary keys). We eliminated all transitive dependencies ($X \to Y \to Z$) by isolating entities into dedicated relations: Doctors reference Department IDs rather than storing department metadata; Beds reference Ward IDs rather than storing per-diem rates; Prescriptions have separate line-item tables for individual drugs.

Q2: Why use PostgreSQL over simple SQLite or MySQL for this project?

Answer: PostgreSQL provides enterprise-grade ACID transactions, custom ENUM domain types, advanced CHECK constraints with regex, transactional DDL, EXCLUDE constraints for range intervals, and scalable PL/pgSQL triggers required in Review 2 for automated bed state management.

Q3: How are Outpatient and Inpatient bills distinguished?

Answer: The bill table includes nullable foreign keys to appointment_id (for outpatient visits) and admission_id (for inpatient stays). Itemized charges (consultation, lab, bed, pharmacy) aggregate into a net total, which is then settled via the 1:N payment receipt table.
