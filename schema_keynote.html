16 Entities Grouped into 6 Operational Subsystems

Every entity maintains single-responsibility semantics to isolate concerns and prevent data redundancy.

DEPARTMENT 1 : N DOCTOR Department employs multiple doctors; doctor belongs to one department (ON DELETE RESTRICT).
DOCTOR 1 : N DOCTOR_SCHEDULE Doctor maintains recurring shift slots; CASCADE deletion removes schedules.
DOCTOR 1 : N APPOINTMENT Doctor attends scheduled appointments; unique (doctor, date, time) prevents double-booking.
PATIENT 1 : N APPOINTMENT Patient books outpatient visits over time; RESTRICT prevents orphan appointment records.
APPOINTMENT 1 : (0..1) CONSULTATION Appointment initiates at most one clinical consultation encounter (UNIQUE appointment_id).
CONSULTATION 1 : N DIAGNOSIS Consultation yields one or more ICD-10 diagnosed conditions (CASCADE).
CONSULTATION 1 : N PRESCRIPTION Consultation produces one or more prescription headers (CASCADE).
PRESCRIPTION 1 : N PRESCRIPTION_ITEM Prescription header details 1..N medication line-items (CASCADE, 1NF normalization).
CONSULTATION 1 : N TEST_ORDER Consultation physician orders lab diagnostics; results tracked with flags.
LAB_TEST 1 : N TEST_ORDER Master lab catalog pricing & code referenced by lab test orders.
WARD 1 : N BED Physical ward houses multiple physical beds (CASCADE).
BED 1 : N ADMISSION Bed accommodates sequential inpatient stays over time (only one active stay).
BILL 1 : N PAYMENT Bill invoice settled in installments via multiple payment receipts (CASCADE).

Relational Integrity Architecture

Foreign Key Referential Actions: CASCADE vs RESTRICT

Strategic application of deletion policies ensures clinical histories cannot be accidentally orphaned while allowing atomic parent-child cleanups.

Policy 1 • ON DELETE CASCADE

Dependent Line Items

Used strictly when a child record has no meaning or legal existence without its parent.

• prescription → prescription_item
• consultation → diagnosis
• ward → bed
• doctor → doctor_schedule
Policy 2 • ON DELETE RESTRICT

Master Clinical Records

Blocks deletion of master entities if any downstream clinical or audit trail references them.

• department → doctor
• doctor → appointment
• patient → admission
• bill → payment
Policy 3 • ON DELETE SET NULL

Financial Audit Retention

Preserves financial ledger rows even if operational encounter records are cancelled or purged.

• appointment → bill (appointment_id)
• admission → bill (admission_id)
Professor Viva Highlight:
"If a doctor leaves the hospital, our schema does not allow DELETE FROM doctor WHERE doctor_id = 1; because ON DELETE RESTRICT protects past medical records and prescriptions from being orphaned. Instead, we toggle is_active = FALSE."
